FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions.
Clear answers.

A helpful starting point for planning your technology project.

Which service should I choose?

Describe the problem you want to solve. Use the contact page to outline your needs, and we can discuss the suitable starting point.

Can I request more than one service?

Yes. A project may combine a website, network setup, cloud tools or training. The combined scope should be agreed before work starts.

How much will my project cost?

Costs depend on requirements, complexity and any equipment or subscriptions needed. Ask for a written quotation for your specific project.

How long will the work take?

A timetable depends on the agreed scope, available information and external dependencies. Agree milestones and responsibilities during planning.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Discuss the support you need when planning the project. Coverage, availability and charges should be stated in the agreed support arrangement.

Will my website work on a phone?

Responsive layouts can be included in the website scope. This demonstration website adapts to phones, tablets and desktop screens.

Can you work with an existing system?

Share details of the system and the issue first. Compatibility, access requirements and the proposed work need to be assessed.

Does this website send my enquiry automatically?

No. The enquiry form prepares a message in your email application once the business email is configured. You review and send it yourself. A server connection is needed for direct website submissions.
