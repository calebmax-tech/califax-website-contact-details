OUR STORY

Start with people.
Then build the technology.

Califax brings websites, software, networks, support, cloud tools, security and practical learning into one technology offering. Over three years, our focus has remained on what technology can help people achieve.

The starting point is your real-world need: a business that wants to be easier to find, a team that needs better connectivity or a learner who wants more confidence using digital tools.

We believe a good solution should be understandable, suitable for its users and planned with everyday use in mind.
